# Ironfront

Ironfront is a multiplayer base-building and survival game. Drop into a persistent server, stake your claim, and carve out your place in an ever-shifting world.

## What is Ironfront?

Ironfront puts you and other players onto shared servers where territory, resources, and reputation are always up for grabs. Every session is shaped by what players choose to build, break, and defend.

- **Build** — Construct bases across a variety of environments, from open plains to rugged, cave-riddled terrain. Design your base for defense, resource production, or just to show off.
- **Command** — Recruit and command troops to defend your territory, escort resources, or push into enemy lines.
- **Raid** — Plan and execute raids on rival structures, breaking through defenses to seize what you need.
- **Form Factions** — Team up with other players, build alliances, and grow your faction's influence across the server.
- **Explore** — Push into new environments to find better resources, strategic terrain, and hidden opportunities before rivals do.

Every server is a living battleground — build up, gear up, and decide whether you'll defend, expand, or raid.

## Modding

Modding support is not yet available in Ironfront, but it's planned for a future release. The goal is to let the community extend and customize the game beyond the base experience — new content, tools, and gameplay tweaks included.

- Mod support is on the roadmap, not yet live.
- Once available, mods will be shareable between players and servers.
- Check back here for modding guides and resources once the feature ships.

## Getting Started

1. Download the latest release from the [Releases](../../releases) page.
2. Extract the release package.
3. Launch the game and join a server, or host your own.
4. Start building, recruiting, and raiding.

## Status

Ironfront is under active development. New features, environments, and balance changes are added regularly — keep an eye on the Releases page for updates.
